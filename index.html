01 / ABOUT ME

Technology with a mission.

I work at the intersection of security information, data, geospatial technology, and digital infrastructure.

In my current role with the joint MONUSCO/UNDSS Security Information and Operations Center in Goma, I support the reception, consolidation, recording, and escalation of security information, staff distress calls, movement monitoring, communications, and operational coordination in accordance with established procedures and supervisory guidance.

My broader technical background includes GIS and cartography, data analysis, databases, dashboards, telecommunications, network systems, monitoring technologies, and the development of digital platforms.
